# IT Security Basics

Rules every employee must follow to protect company data.

## Passwords

Passwords must have at least 12 characters and be unique for each system. Use the company password manager to generate and store them. Passwords are never shared by email, chat or paper notes.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and Odoo. Use the authenticator app on your phone. SMS codes are only accepted as a temporary fallback for up to 7 days.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour. IT will lock the device remotely and reset your credentials. Do not wait until the next working day.

## Phishing

Do not click links or open attachments from unexpected emails. Use the "Report phishing" button of the mail client to forward suspicious messages to the security team. If you already clicked a link or typed your password, contact IT immediately.

## Data classification

Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer contracts and financial statements are Confidential. Payroll and personal data are Restricted and may only be shared with authorised people through approved systems.

## Software installation

Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed on company devices. Requests for new software are made through the IT helpdesk and reviewed within 5 working days.

## Leaving the company

On the last working day all devices are returned to IT and access is removed within 24 hours.
