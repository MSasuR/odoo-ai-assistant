# Quotation and Order Process

This document explains how the sales team creates quotations and turns them into confirmed orders in Odoo.

## Creating a quotation

Open the Sales app, click New, choose the customer and add the product lines. Every quotation has a validity of 30 days by default. Use the "Send by email" button so the customer receives a PDF and a link to accept online.

## Discount approval

Salespeople can grant discounts of up to 10% on their own. A discount above 10% and up to 25% needs the approval of the sales manager. A discount above 25% needs the approval of the sales director and must include a written justification in the chatter of the quotation.

## Payment terms

The standard payment term is 30 days from the invoice date. Customers with a credit limit above 50,000 EUR can be offered 45 or 60 days, but only with the approval of Finance. New customers pay 50% in advance on orders above 10,000 EUR.

## Confirming an order

When the customer accepts, click Confirm. The quotation becomes a sales order, the delivery is created and the warehouse is notified. A confirmed order cannot be edited directly: use the "Unlock" option and record the reason.

## Contracts

Orders above 50,000 EUR require a signed contract uploaded to the order before confirmation. The legal team provides the contract templates in the shared drive.

## Order states

- Quotation (draft): still editable and not binding.
- Sales Order (sale): confirmed and binding.
- Cancelled: the customer declined or the quotation expired.

## Following up

Quotations that are not answered within 7 days should be followed up by phone. The salesperson logs the outcome as an activity on the quotation.
