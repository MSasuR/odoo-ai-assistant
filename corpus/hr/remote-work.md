# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Traders supports flexible work. This document describes who can work remotely and under which conditions.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Roles that require on-site presence, such as the warehouse and reception, are not eligible. Managers may approve exceptions for temporary situations.

## Core hours

Regardless of location, everyone must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 in their local time zone. Meetings are scheduled inside this window whenever possible.

## Home office equipment allowance

Each remote employee receives a one-time allowance of 400 EUR to buy a desk, chair or monitor. The allowance can be claimed once every 3 years by submitting the receipts as an expense claim. Laptops and phones are always provided by the company and are not covered by this allowance.

## Internet and phone costs

A monthly stipend of 30 EUR covers internet costs for employees working remotely at least 3 days per week. It is paid with the regular payroll and does not require receipts.

## Working from another country

Working from abroad for more than 14 days per year needs prior approval from HR and Finance because of tax and social-security implications. Requests must be submitted at least 30 days before the trip.

## Security requirements

Remote work must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from public networks. Company devices must not be shared with family members, and screens must be locked when unattended.
