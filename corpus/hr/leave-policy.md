# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Traders, a fictional company used as sample data for this project.

## Annual leave entitlement

Every full-time employee receives 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a proportional amount. Leave accrues monthly, so an employee who joins in July receives roughly half of the yearly entitlement.

## Requesting leave

Leave is requested in the Odoo Time Off app. Create a new request, choose the leave type and the dates, and submit it. Requests for more than five consecutive working days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance. Shorter absences should be requested at least 2 working days in advance, except in emergencies.

## Approval

The direct manager approves or refuses the request within 3 working days. If the manager does not answer within that time, the request is escalated to the department head. Approved leave appears in the shared team calendar. A refused request always includes a reason written by the manager.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used before 31 March. Any other unused days expire on 31 December and are not paid out, except where local law requires it.

## Sick leave

Report sick leave to your manager before the start of your working day. A medical certificate is required when the absence lasts more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave is recorded in Odoo with the leave type "Sick Leave" and does not reduce the annual leave balance.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year can be granted for personal reasons with the approval of both the direct manager and the HR department. Longer periods need written approval from the HR director.

## Checking your balance

Your remaining balance is shown in the Time Off app, on the dashboard card of each leave type. You can also ask the assistant for your current balance at any time.

## Cancelling a request

An approved request can be cancelled up to the day before it starts. After that date, contact HR, who will adjust the balance manually.
