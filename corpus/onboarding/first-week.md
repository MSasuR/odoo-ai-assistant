# First Week Checklist

What a new employee can expect during the first five working days.

## Before day one

HR sends the welcome email with the address, the arrival time and the name of your mentor. IT prepares your laptop and creates your accounts for email and Odoo.

## Day one

You meet your manager and your mentor, receive your laptop and access badge, and take the security induction of about 45 minutes. Set up multi-factor authentication before doing anything else.

## Odoo access

Your Odoo user is created by IT. Access to additional apps, such as Sales or Accounting, is requested by your manager through the IT helpdesk and is granted within 2 working days. You can always see your own leave balance and payslip requests without extra permissions.

## Mandatory training

During the first two weeks you complete three short online courses: data protection, information security and the code of conduct. Each takes about 30 minutes and is tracked in the learning platform.

## Mentor programme

Your mentor is an experienced colleague from a different team. You meet once a week for 30 minutes during the first three months to ask any question about how things work.

## Probation period

The probation period lasts 3 months. Your manager holds a review meeting at the end of the first month and another at the end of the third month.

## Who to ask

- Payroll and leave questions: the HR team.
- Laptop or account problems: the IT helpdesk.
- Expense claims: the Finance team.
