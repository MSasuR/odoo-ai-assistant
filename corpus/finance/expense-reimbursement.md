# Expense Reimbursement

How to claim business expenses at Northwind Traders.

## Submitting a claim

Create an expense in the Odoo Expenses app, attach a photo or PDF of the receipt and choose the category. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 30 days are only accepted with a written explanation approved by Finance.

## Receipts

A receipt is mandatory for every expense above 25 EUR. Below that amount a receipt is not required, but the description must say what the expense was for.

## Daily allowances

While travelling on business, meals are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per day in Europe and up to 60 EUR per day elsewhere. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Travel

Flights above 500 EUR and hotel stays above 150 EUR per night need approval from the manager before booking. Train travel in second class is preferred for journeys under 4 hours.

## Approval and payment

The direct manager approves the claim, then Finance verifies the receipts. Approved claims are paid on the 15th of the following month together with the regular payroll.

## Personal vehicle

Business trips with a personal car are reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre. Record the start, end and purpose of every trip.
