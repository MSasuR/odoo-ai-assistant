# Invoicing and Collections

This document describes how customer invoices are issued and how overdue payments are collected.

## Issuing invoices

Invoices are created from the confirmed sales order, after the goods are delivered or the service is completed. Only the finance team can post an invoice. A posted invoice cannot be edited: to correct it, issue a credit note and create a new invoice.

## Payment terms and due dates

The due date is calculated from the invoice date and the payment term of the customer, 30 days by default. The invoice must show the company bank account and the payment reference.

## Payment reminders

Overdue invoices follow a fixed reminder schedule:

1. 7 days after the due date: a friendly reminder email.
2. 15 days after the due date: a formal reminder by email with the invoice attached.
3. 30 days after the due date: a phone call by the collections team and a notification to the account manager.
4. 60 days after the due date: the account is put on credit hold and no new orders are confirmed.

## Disputed invoices

If a customer disputes an invoice, log the reason in the chatter and mark the invoice with the "Disputed" tag. Disputed invoices are paused in the reminder schedule for up to 20 days while sales and finance investigate.

## Credit notes

A credit note is issued for returns, price corrections and cancelled services. Credit notes above 5,000 EUR need the approval of the finance manager before they are posted.

## Partial payments

Partial payments are registered against the invoice and the remaining balance keeps its original due date. The reminder schedule applies to the outstanding balance only.

## Foreign currency

Invoices in a foreign currency are converted with the exchange rate of the invoice date. Exchange differences are posted automatically when the payment is registered.
